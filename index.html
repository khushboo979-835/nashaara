Roadmap to Freedom

Our Proven 4-Step Recovery Process

Every patient follows a structured scientific trajectory designed to restore physical stamina, mental clarity, and family harmony.

STEP 1

Medical Assessment

Comprehensive physical checkup, psychiatric evaluation, blood reports, and tailored rehabilitation planning.

STEP 2

Safe Detoxification

24/7 medically managed withdrawal management to flush toxins smoothly with minimum physical distress.

STEP 3

Therapy & Counseling

One-on-one psychological counseling, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), 12-step program, and daily yoga sessions.

STEP 4

Social Re-Integration

Family counseling, life skills training, relapse prevention workshops, and post-discharge follow-up support.
