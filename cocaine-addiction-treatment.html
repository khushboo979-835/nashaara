Cocaine & Stimulant Addiction Treatment

Cocaine and stimulant pills cause extreme cardiovascular strain, dopamine depletion, paranoia, and severe crashes. We offer dedicated cognitive rehabilitation.

At Nasha Mukti Kendra Ara (located at Kaushalya Samay Hospital, Ara, Bhojpur, Bihar), our rehabilitation protocols are structured by consultant psychiatrists, physicians, and experienced de-addiction counselors to ensure complete, confidential recovery.

Clinical Treatment Protocol

1. Cardiovascular & Neural Stabilization

Hospital care to stabilize blood pressure, manage tachycardia, and calm acute agitation safely.

2. Cognitive Restructuring & Craving Management

Intensive psychotherapy to retrain dopamine response mechanisms and extinguish impulsive triggers.

3. Lifestyle Re-Engineering

Healthy habits, emotional self-regulation, and continuous aftercare monitoring to prevent future cravings.
